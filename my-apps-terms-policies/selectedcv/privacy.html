SelectedCV Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority

Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to SelectedCV — a professional CV maker designed with your privacy in mind. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1

Information We Collect

SelectedCV is built as an offline-first application. Most of your CV data is stored locally on your device. However, to provide certain features, we use trusted third-party services.

CV & Personal Data

Your name, education, work experience, and photos are stored locally on your device using secure databases.

Device & Usage Data

Collected by Google AdMob and Firebase for ads, crash reporting, and app functionality (e.g., Advertising ID).

Voice Input Data

When using Voice Input, audio is processed by your device's speech recognition service. We do not store your voice.

🔒 We do NOT sell, rent, or share your personal CV content to any third parties.

2

How We Use Your Information

The data we collect is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To display relevant advertisements through Google AdMob
  • To process in-app purchases (e.g., "Remove Ads") via Google Play Billing
  • To authenticate users and verify purchases securely using Firebase
  • To improve app performance, fix bugs, and prevent crashes
  • To enable voice-to-text features using your device's microphone
3

Third-Party Services

SelectedCV integrates the following trusted third-party services, which may collect data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements
  • Google Play Billing — for secure in-app purchases
  • Firebase (Google) — for secure purchase verification, backend functions, and crash analytics
  • Google Speech Recognition — for voice-to-text input

You can review Google's Privacy Policy here: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4

Permissions We Request

To provide you with the best experience, SelectedCV requests the following permissions:

  • Storage (Files & Media) — to save and export your CV as a PDF file.
  • Camera & Photos — to add a profile picture to your CV.
  • Microphone — to allow voice dictation for faster CV creation.
  • Internet — to load ads and process secure in-app purchases.

All permissions are optional and can be disabled in your device settings without affecting offline features.

5

Data Security & Retention

Your CV data is stored locally using Hive database, protected by your device's built-in security. Any data transmitted to third parties (like Firebase or Google Play) is sent over secure HTTPS encrypted connections.

We retain purchase verification data only as long as necessary to provide you with premium features. You can request data deletion by contacting us.

6

Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • Delete individual CVs directly within the app.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data immediately.
  • Manage ad personalization via your Google Account settings.
  • Request Account/Data Deletion: If you have made an in-app purchase or logged in, you can email us at cvbhayya@gmail.com to request deletion of your purchase/authentication data from our Firebase servers.
7

Children's Privacy

SelectedCV is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

8

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
